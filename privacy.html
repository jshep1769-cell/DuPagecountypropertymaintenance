Privacy Policy

DuPage Property Care Guide is an independent information and telephone-inquiry website about snow removal, landscaping, and seasonal property care in DuPage County, Illinois. This policy describes how information may be handled when you visit this website or call the number shown on it.

Last updated: November 19, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains the types of information we may receive, why we use it, and when it may be shared. The website helps people make inquiries about property maintenance services; it does not perform snow removal or landscaping directly.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with the website and telephone number, information may include:

  • Information you provide: Your name, contact details, property location, service needs, and other details you choose to share during an inquiry.
  • Telephone information: Your phone number or caller ID, call date, time, duration, and information you choose to leave in a voicemail.
  • Inquiry details: Information about the work you are seeking, such as driveway clearing, walkway access, or landscaping questions.
  • Basic technical information: Information that a website host or technical service may process to operate or secure the site, such as an IP address, browser type, or general usage and error logs.

The current website does not include an online inquiry form. Please do not provide sensitive personal or financial information unless it is necessary for a service inquiry.

3. How We Use Information

We may use relevant information to understand and respond to inquiries, help connect interested callers with suitable independent service providers, facilitate requested discussions about services, assess the quality of inquiries, maintain and improve the website, prevent misuse, and meet applicable legal obligations.

Calling this website does not by itself authorize unrelated marketing calls, text messages, or emails.

4. Lead Generation and Third-Party Service Providers

This website operates for lead-generation and referral purposes. We are not a snow removal or landscaping contractor and do not perform the services described on this site.

When you call, your inquiry or relevant details you provide may be referred or connected to an independent third-party service provider that may be able to discuss your needs. The website operator may receive compensation for generating or referring qualifying inquiries.

Independent service providers decide what services they offer, where they operate, their prices, and whether they accept a job. They may handle your information under their own privacy practices. A call does not guarantee a referral, appointment, quote, or completed service.

5. Call Tracking, Routing, and Recording

Telephone inquiries may be handled through third-party telecommunications services. Those services may process caller ID, call times and duration, routing details, and voicemail messages if you choose to leave one. This information may help us respond to or assess inquiries.

We do not represent that every call is recorded. If call audio recording is used, appropriate notice and consent will be provided where required by law. Any provider to whom a call is connected may also have its own calling and privacy practices.

6. Cookies and Website Analytics

The current website's HTML does not include a dedicated advertising pixel or analytics script, and it does not provide a cookie-based preference or contact form. The website hosting service may process standard technical information needed to deliver, maintain, or secure pages.

If we add analytics, advertising technology, or optional cookies later, we will revise this policy as appropriate to describe those practices.

7. Information Sharing

Information connected with your inquiry may be shared with an appropriate independent service provider for the purpose of discussing the service you requested. Technical information may also be handled by the companies providing website hosting, telephone routing, voicemail, or related operational services. We may disclose information when required by applicable law or to address misuse or security concerns.

Because referrals are part of this website's purpose, you should not assume that inquiry information will remain solely with this website operator.

8. Data Security and Retention

We aim to use reasonable safeguards suited to the information and services involved. However, no website or telephone system can promise complete security.

Information may be kept for as long as reasonably necessary to handle inquiries, manage operations, resolve concerns, or meet applicable requirements. Actual storage and retention practices may also depend on the technical and telecommunications providers involved.

9. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on applicable law and the information we maintain, you may be able to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information. To raise a privacy question or request, call (630) 534-0189 and leave a message if prompted. Please say that the message concerns privacy and provide only the contact details needed for a response.

We may need to verify a request and may not control information independently held by a referred service provider. Requests will be considered in light of applicable law and the information available to us.

10. Third-Party Websites and Services

Third-party websites, telephone providers, and independently operated service businesses may have their own privacy notices and information-handling practices. We are not responsible for their separate policies or service decisions. Review their terms before sharing additional information or booking work.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if our website, telephone arrangements, referral process, or information practices change. The latest version will be posted on this page with a revised update date.
